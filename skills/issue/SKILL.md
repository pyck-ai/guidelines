# Issue guideline

The guideline is [issues.md](issues.md) in this directory. Read it in full before you start. Without a local copy, read <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pyck-ai/guidelines/main/docs/issues.md>.

## Checklist

- The issue is classified as defect, feature or task, as the guideline describes.
- Issues are small and focused, not meta-tickets; parent and sub-issue links are used as the guideline describes.
- The matching form is used and every required field is filled.
- No em or en dashes.
- Fill the matching issue form as is (forms under `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`, for example [`1-defect.yml`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pyck-ai/.github/main/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/1-defect.yml), with `2-feature.yml` and `3-task.yml` beside it); never restate its fields.
