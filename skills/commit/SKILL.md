# Commit and branch guideline

The guideline is [git.md](git.md) in this directory. Read it in full before you start. Without a local copy, read <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pyck-ai/guidelines/main/docs/git.md>.

## Checklist

- Subject, body and footer follow the guideline.
- The message stands on its own: what shipped and why it was done this way.
- Branch names follow "Branch naming".
- No em or en dashes in the message.
